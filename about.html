About Harmony Kaboom

A Kampala agency for premium brand experiences.

Who we are

We are a Kampala-based team working across events, brand marketing, social events and content creation. Our focus is introducing premium brands to Ugandan guests through experiences rather than conventional advertising alone.

Every experience we design has a story to tell, a setting that suits it and content that lets the moment travel further.

  • Local knowledge of Kampala venues, hotels and restaurants
  • Events, marketing and content handled by one team
  • Guest lists curated for fit, not for volume
  • Pilot first, then scale based on results

How we work

Understand the brand

Its story, its product and the guest it is meant for.

Design the experience

Format, venue, food, guest list and content plan.

Run and review

Deliver the event, measure the response and decide what comes next.
